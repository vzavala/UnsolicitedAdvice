# There Are Many Career Paths

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [January 25, 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7156424422420094976/).

When we imagine a scientist, we imagine a person in the lab, doing field trails, or doing coding/theory. 

But there are so many career paths that you can follow after your degree; some that I have seen in my career are policy making, business consulting, patent law, scientific communication, grant management, chef, video game developer, art preservation/restoration, early childhood education. 

A degree in science/technology provides a powerful framework for thinking about problems, you can combine that skillset with other disciplines to come up with new approaches to solve problems in different domains.
