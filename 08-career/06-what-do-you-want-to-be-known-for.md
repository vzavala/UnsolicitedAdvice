# What Do You Want to Be Known For?

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [January 7, 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7414776725727002624/).

When I was interviewing for faculty jobs, a Professor asked me: “What do you want to be known for?"

Sounds like an easy question, but at the time I did not have a solid answer. I came up with an answer that was good enough (I guess) but I was not fully pleased with this. To this day I think about this question quite regularly and I use this as a way to guide what I do and what motivates me. 

There are many ways to address this question, but I think it is important to have some sort of vision/plan that guides you and your work. It is easy to shoot into so many directions and get into paths that deviate from what you originally planned for; having a guide helps you re-center and correct course. I also think it is important to think strategically about what reputation/expertise you want to build, as this will define your core capabilities and will be beneficial when establishing collaborations and when raising funding.
