# Becoming an Independent Researcher

> *More advice (not part of the series).* Posted on LinkedIn on [May 21, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7330751623771643904/).

This was the 1st paper of my career as an independent scientist (without any of my undergraduate, PhD, or postdoc advisors). 

To be honest, it was a scary feeling to publish something without my advisors (the guardrails were off), but this was such an important step that made me realize that I was capable of coming up with my own ideas and that such ideas had merit. I felt super proud after getting this published. 

Becoming an independent researcher is a difficult but important step. You grow significantly by developing your own ideas and by identifying the unique angle/niche that you bring into the community.
