# Get Involved in Teaching

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [September 29, 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7246150107656544256/).

Find opportunities to get involved in teaching/education, even if you are not considering a career in academia. 

Teaching topics/concepts related to your research will force you to go deep into the fundamentals, as teaching requires a clear train of thought. When you go deep into the fundamentals, you will also often trigger new ideas for your research. 

Teaching will also force you to think about your research from different angles (depending on the audience that you are targeting). For instance, explaining a concept to an undergraduate student is different than explaining the same concept to a high-school student, to the public, or to an investor. Targeting different audiences will improve your scientific communication skills. 

You can get involved in teaching/education by developing tutorials and educational resources (e.g., slides, software, videos). These resources are invaluable for the scientific community (academic and industry); such resources can also help the community learn how to apply your research to their problems.
