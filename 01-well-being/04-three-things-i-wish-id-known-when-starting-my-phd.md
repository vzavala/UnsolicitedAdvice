# Three Things I Wish I'd Known When Starting My PhD

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists (particularly those starting their PhD).* Posted on LinkedIn on [September 3, 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7236533047527890944/).

Here are 3 things I wish I could go back in time and tell myself when I was starting my PhD: 

- The PhD is truly an exciting time from a professional and personal stand-point. You will learn a lot about science and about yourself and you will be growing intellectually and personally at an exponential rate. Things will be moving fast; try to stay present and enjoy the process. 

- You will be busy/excited but should find time to rest; a PhD is a marathon, not a sprint. Remember that a PhD is intellectually challenging and you will be pushing your brain/mind to new limits. It is difficult to anticipate when you will "hit a wall" and face burnout; try to pace yourself, eat healthy, exercise, don't forget about your hobbies, stay connected with friends/family, and sleep well (VERY well). 

- As with any big shift in life, there will be fear/uncertainty/anxiety attached to excitement. This is absolutely normal and, trust me, most of your classmates feel the same way. Chat openly with your peers and mentors about your struggles and fears, it is good to realize that one is not alone.
