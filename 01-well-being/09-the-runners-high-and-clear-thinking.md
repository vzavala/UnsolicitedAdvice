# The Runner's High and Clear Thinking

> Posted on LinkedIn on [July 26, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7354929061493686275/).

There is an interesting phenomenon that runners often experience called "runner's high." 

This is a state that you experience after a few miles of running and is difficult to describe. The way it feels to me is that your anxiety disappears, you relax, and you enter a form of "synergy/synchrony" between your body and thoughts. Once you enter that state, your thoughts become more "streamlined" and you start generating ideas. 

For many people like me, running is an effective way of controlling anxiety and of clearing your head. When I start running, I often have so much noise in my head due to anxiety that makes it difficult to formulate thoughts and ideas. I have generated many research ideas and solved many problems while running...  I wish I would have embraced running more when I was a PhD student :)
