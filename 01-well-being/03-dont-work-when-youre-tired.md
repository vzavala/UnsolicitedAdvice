# Don't Work When You're Tired

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [April 19, 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7451453557406334976/).

Avoid working when you feel tired.

I often feel that the work I do when I am tired is a bit of a waste of time, because I often make mistakes and need to add time to correct them. Also, I don’t particularly enjoy the work I do when I feel tired. 

When you are well rested you get much more done, your ideas are sharper, and you enjoy work more. 

One strategy I use to keep myself in check is to have a clear set of goals to accomplish in a day. When I hit those goals, I stop and rest (as opposed to keep squeezing more tasks in my day). 

Rest ultimately boosts productivity, it is time well invested.
