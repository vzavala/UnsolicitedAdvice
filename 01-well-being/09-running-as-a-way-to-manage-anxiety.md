# Running as a Way to Manage Anxiety

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [May 25, 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7464792802758070274/).

Throughout my adult life, long-distance running has been my most effective approach to deal with my recurrent anxiety. 

Running not only naturally helps you balance chemicals in your brain that are responsible for anxiety, but it also gives you time to monitor your thoughts and to control them.

As an example, in long runs, the brain naturally activates "survival mechanisms" and starts sending you self-defeating messages to stop running. Some thoughts that I usually get are: "this is crazy", "stop, why are you doing this?", "you are not that good anyway." 

After many years of running, I have figured out ways to control these unhealthy thoughts; I apply similar techniques to control thoughts on a day to day basis. You can literally retrain/rewire your brain differently; it takes a lot of patience and consistency, but it is doable.
