# Sketch an Outline Before You Write

> *More advice (not part of the series).* Posted on LinkedIn on [September 1, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7368295272369569793/).

I have said this before, but I will say it again… as I feel that students get very anxious about  technical writing and presentations.

It is super helpful to sketch an outline of your paper, talk, or poster before you begin writing or putting the slides together.

Writing without a sketch is like showing up to a vacation without prior planning… things can work out but the pain will be significant :) 

I also feel that sketching your ideas helps make the whole process more tractable and reduces anxiety, because it helps structure your thoughts. The sketch can be rough and imprecise, just enough to get you in the right direction. Your sketch can be graphical or a collection of bullet points and can be on paper or on the computer.
