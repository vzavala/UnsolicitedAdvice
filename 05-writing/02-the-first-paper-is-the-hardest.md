# The First Paper Is the Hardest

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [April 4, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7313717545696407552/).

It is an awesome feeling to get the 1st paper of your PhD published. The sense of accomplishment is unmatched. I remember showing my paper to my parents because I felt so proud :) 

Writing that 1st paper, however, can feel daunting because this is a significant endeavor (you are learning many new skills simultaneously as you go). But, trust me, it gets exponentially easier with the 2nd and 3rd paper. Technical writing is an acquired skill and you learn quickly. 

If you are feeling stuck, keep pushing and don't give up. You will feel *so* accomplished and relieved after you get it done. Also, try to enjoy the process of learning to write; I have so many fond memories of writing my 1st paper.
