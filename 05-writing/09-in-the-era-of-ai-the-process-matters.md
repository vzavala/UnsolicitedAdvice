# In the Era of AI, the Process Matters

> Posted on LinkedIn on [October 12, 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7515417138354982913/).

In the era of AI, thinking about the process (and not just the product) is essential. 

You do not write a paper just for the sake of creating something; you write it to gain skills, to think and organize your thoughts, to philosophize, to communicate your experience. The paper (the product) is important, but the creation process is even more so. 

Also, part of understanding a product is understanding the process that generated it. This is why it is important to clearly understand how AI is arriving at a solution and to carefully scrutinize it and validate it (this requires domain expertise). If you do not understand the process, you cannot improve the product. In other words, you cannot separate the product from the process, these are inherently intertwined.
