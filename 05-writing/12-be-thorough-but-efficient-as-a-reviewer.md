# Be Thorough but Efficient as a Reviewer

> *More advice (not part of the series).* Posted on LinkedIn on [July 15, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7350815291565617156/).

If you are serving as a reviewer for a journal, take the following into consideration.

Every time we request corrections from the authors (even if minor), we are requesting time/effort from the editorial staff and from the authors to re-process the manuscript.

I am saying this because sometimes I see papers that are sent out for review 3-4 times and often for super minor corrections that could have been identified in the first 1-2 rounds of review. 

I do think it is important to be through and careful, but at the same time it is important to be efficient.
