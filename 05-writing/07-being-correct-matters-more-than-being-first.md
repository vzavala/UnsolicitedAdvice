# Being Correct Matters More Than Being First

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [July 30, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7356271908037775360/).

It is important to be the first to publish a result, but it is perhaps more important to do things correctly.

Oftentimes we feel the pressure/urge of publishing our results because we feel that we need to be the first ones. However, in my opinion, work that is done carefully is more impactful in the long run. 

You can get to a race late, but you can still make a substantial contribution that expands what everyone else has already done... this also has substantial impact (if not more).

It is also quite unlikely that a couple of research groups will have *exactly* the same ideas or approaches to a problem; there are often enough differences in motivation, application, and perspective to claim novelty. We are often looking at the same beast from different angles. 

It is also quite unlikely that we are truly the first ones to think about a problem or to solve a problem. In the words of Goethe... "Everything has been thought of, the problem is to think of it again." 

Throughout my career, I have consistently found that many of the "new" ideas that I have had are inspired or build on preliminary results that someone else has already established (sometimes decades ago). I do not let this fact deter me; if anything, I often find some comfort in finding out that someone else has had a similar idea, and that I am not crazy :)
