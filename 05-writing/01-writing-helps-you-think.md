# Writing Helps You Think

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [December 2, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7401635042026037248/).

Technical writing goes beyond communicating our ideas to other people.

Technical writing helps you structure your thoughts and it thus helps you organize, compress, and store information. I have vivid memories of every paper, letter, post, report, etc. that I have written. 

In other words, technical writing help us process, distill, and internalize our ideas. Writing helps me think and communicate my own ideas to myself :)
