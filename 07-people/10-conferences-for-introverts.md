# Conferences for Introverts

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [November 8, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7392734441485459456/).

As an introvert, I have always struggled navigating conferences. But, after many years of being in this business, I have noticed the following trends. 

On the first day, it is difficult to get comfortable hanging out with people that you do not know and it is difficult to interact with large groups. But, after the first day, I get comfortable and naturally immerse in the environment. From time to time, I do need to take a break, but interactions flow more naturally. 

Also, after interacting with the same community for many years, I more naturally flow into it. In addition, after participating in so many conferences, I have developed strategies to facilitate my integration into new environments; as such, new environments do not feel as threatening. 

I mention this because I feel that there is a misconception that introverts are “antisocial”; that is not true, we can be extremely social, but it takes us a bit more time to adapt to social environments.
