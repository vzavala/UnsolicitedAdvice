# Ask People About Their Career Paths

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [June 23, 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7210657045119660032/).

Ask your fellow scientists about their career paths and life stories. How did they end up deciding to get a PhD? Why a PhD in this specific topic? Why going to another country?...

Throughout my career, I have come across life stories of people that are pretty remarkable. You realize that, in many cases, decisions are not made by us (they are made for us), you find that there are elements of pure randomness/chance, and you find that career paths are often nonlinear (e.g., going from industry to academia or the other way around). As an example, I have a friend that majored in a topic in college, worked in the corporate world for ~10 years, and decided to go back to college to major in a completely different topic and eventually getting a PhD in such topic (completely shifting their career path). 

I love learning about all of these career paths and life stories, it helps me appreciate the resilience of people and to understand what motivates/drives them. Having the opportunity to meet people with such interesting and diverse backgrounds is one of my favorite things of being a scientist. 

I also think that we should all share our life stories more openly, I think this can help us connect to and inspire younger generations.
