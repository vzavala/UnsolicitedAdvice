# What Should You Call Your Professor?

> Posted on LinkedIn on [September 2, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7368774556330725376/).

Students often hesitate to call professors by their name… they often call me Prof or Dr. 

I can understand this, it took me a solid year to call my PhD advisor by his name. 

This is a sign of respect but can also represent a perception of hierarchy that might hinder communication. 

Everyone is different. I often tell students that they can call me whatever they feel comfortable with… my kids call me “bro” and “dude” and we get along just fine…
