# College Is Also About Networks

> *More advice (not part of the series).* Posted on LinkedIn on [August 23, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7365037444573880320/).

I feel that an under-appreciated aspect of college education is access to networks. 

We often think that attending college is about gaining knowledge/skills in the classroom or labs; this is definitely an essential component but, as a student, you also gain knowledge/skills via interactions with your classmates, with your friends, and with the entire university community. Attending college provides access to such a rich social network and ecosystem. 

As a college student, I was fortunate to connect with so many talented young people from so many different backgrounds; part of my growth during that time was all the stories, perspectives, and experiences we shared (inside and outside the classroom). 

Conversations with my classmates helped me identify what I liked and what I wanted to do after graduation.
