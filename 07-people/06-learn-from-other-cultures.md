# Learn from Other Cultures

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [May 13, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7328099441033256962/).

An important aspect of scientific preparation is to interact with people from other cultures (e.g., via travel, student organizations, exchange programs, and conferences). 

You learn so much by talking to people from different backgrounds, as their culture/experiences/values often influence what problems they decide to work on and how they approach problems.
