# Self-Awareness Is Underrated

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [June 28, 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7477022988173778944/).

A highly underrated ability is that of *self-awareness.* Self-awareness is the ability to monitor our emotions/actions and the impact that they have on other people. 

In my view, self-awareness is at the core of our ability to form, coordinate, and participate in teams of people. Self-awareness helps build empathy, helps understand why we are doing what we are doing , helps understand how our actions can hinder or stimulate other people, helps monitor the well-being of ourselves and of our colleagues, and ultimately helps us understand how to best help and mentor people. 

Self-awareness is also important to help keep our egos in check and helps us monitor and prevent unhealthy feelings and actions (e.g., extreme competition, insecurities, bullying, microagressions). In many cases, unhealthy actions are unintentional, but the fact that there is no intent does not mean that there is no impact on others. This is why it is important to be self-aware and think about how our actions affect others. 

More broadly, I feel that emotional intelligence is a highly underrated skill. Sometimes people are not successful because they lack technical ability, but because they cannot relate and work with others.
