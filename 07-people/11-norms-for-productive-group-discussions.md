# Norms for Productive Group Discussions

> Posted on LinkedIn on [October 21, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7386386719333539840/).

As I get older, I find myself in positions in which I have to coordinate discussions between large groups of people. Managing such conversations can be challenging if there are no established norms/bounds of behavior. 

I like this slide that aims to remind a group of what are constructive/productive group behaviors and what are unproductive ones. I think we should always be reminded of these in order to have healthy debate and productive discussions. Ultimately, a group should aim to cooperate to *learn* together; this is not a competition to see who has the best ideas or who is the smartest.
