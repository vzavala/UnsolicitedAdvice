# Don't Neglect the Social Side of Science

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [March 2, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7301987157676630016/).

When I was a PhD student I did not fully appreciate the social and management aspects of science (e.g., importance of communication, team and project leadership/management, collaborations). These are things that I discovered and learned along the way in my professional career, but I think the path would have been much smoother if I would have been a bit more educated on the subject :) 

I cannot stress enough how important it is for you to be able to communicate effectively (verbally and in writing), I cannot stress enough how important it is to get involved in leadership/management roles (e.g., student organizations, outreach), and I cannot stress enough how important it is to be a good collaborator. I also think that it is important to take an active leadership role in your own PhD project (e.g., learn to develop strategic directions and manage its products). 

All these "soft" skills will be super helpful in your professional career; they will make you more self-aware, strategic, empathic, and reliable. Scientific projects are difficult to execute without proper leadership/management skills and without people that are good team players.
