# Mentoring Is Built on Mutual Trust

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [March 20, 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7440854052868915200/).

The relationship between a mentor and a mentee is based on mutual trust.

The mentee needs to trust that the mentor will be open to ideas and will be reliable/responsive when needed. 

Likewise, the mentor needs to trust that the mentee will be open to ideas and will be reliable/responsive when needed.
