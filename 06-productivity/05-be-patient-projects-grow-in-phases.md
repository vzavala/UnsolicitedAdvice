# Be Patient: Projects Grow in Phases

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [July 1, 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7213557114848337920/).

Be patient when conducting research, particularly when it involves multi-disciplinary collaborations/projects. 

Research progress often feels super slow at the beginning of a project; the team members are learning new concepts/skills, members are getting to know each other and their capabilities, experiments/models take time to set up, and so on. 

However, like anything in life, a project exhibits growth phases; at the beginning it grows slowly but then it can reach an exponential growth phase (once all the elements come together and the team reaches synergy). I have witnessed this time and time again when managing large research projects. 

Also, in order to foster growth, you need to provide adequate resources and an environment that help the team interact, exchange ideas, and achieve synergy/convergence. You also need to contribute to ensure that the resources and the environment are adequate/healthy.
