# Plan Your Day in Time Slots

> Posted on LinkedIn on [August 14, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7361732482649509888/).

Someone once told me a story of a professor that used to be in the military and that planned/scheduled their days using time slots of 30 min. 

I found this story amusing but it stuck with me, as I tend to operate that way (not that extreme, but similar). I am quite rigorous about allocating time to specific tasks and to try to follow a plan. It is not always possible (due to unforeseen disturbances), but I find it useful to mitigate anxiety and to try to stay mentally healthy. 

I started working this way because, when I was a PhD student, I did not have a structure; as a result, I used to "drift" into endless rabbit holes and I used to work unhealthy hours.
