# Learn to Say No

> Posted on LinkedIn on [July 28, 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7487872426744115201/).

Saying "No" is 1000 times more difficult than saying "Yes." 

I have learned this lesson the hard way; I feel that I am often overcommitting. 

Saying "Yes" to a request feels like you are pleasing someone (e.g., you feel good), while saying "No" feels like you are disappointing someone (e.g., you feel guilty). 

But your mental peace/energy should always come first; sometimes saying "No" to someone is saying "Yes" to yourself.
