# Time Management Is an Underrated Skill

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [August 29, 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7234738258805415938/).

When I was a PhD student, I somehow had the idea that scientists were absent-minded entities that randomly came up with ideas and papers, that they had zero work structure and organization skills.

Yes, there is a bit of truth to this :) but in reality you do have to be organized and plan your research carefully if you want to be productive. Being unstructured, disorganized, and playful sometimes promotes creative thinking, but it is also not sustainable and can be stressful. 

For instance, most research projects have deadlines and occur collaboratively; as such, people rely on your results and progress. Also, even if you are working alone, it is good to have some form of structure or expectation/plan of where you want your research to take you (otherwise you might end up getting stuck in rabbit holes or have too many unproductive ideas running in your head). 

Throughout my career, I have found that time management is truly an underrated skill; it is important to manage your time/effort in order to stay productive, have a good work-life balance, and stay sane.
