# Think Before You Hit "Solve"

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists doing computational research.* Posted on LinkedIn on [February 2, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7291827234682585088/).

There is A LOT that you should do BEFORE hitting that “solve” button and execute your computational model. 

I keep thinking about the “golden years” of computation in which people used punched cards for coding. People had to meticulously derive their model/algorithm on paper, transcribe it carefully to the card, and check every line carefully to make sure there were no bugs (literally and figuratively). 

I think this process, even if slow, made people think very carefully/strategically, helped them anticipate the possible outcomes resulting from the execution their model, and helped them think about the value of such outcomes. In other words, executing a model was a carefully crafted experiment. 

I feel that nowadays we take computation so much for granted that we do not think/strategize much before executing our models. We have the ability to experiment so much that we do not see the need to plan ahead. I think this limits our ability to think abstractly and to extract valuable insights from our models.
