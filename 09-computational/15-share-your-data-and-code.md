# Share Your Data and Code

> *More advice (not part of the series).* Posted on LinkedIn on [June 19, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7341520184827924481/).

I strongly urge everyone working on AI/ML to share your data/code with your manuscripts. 

I personally see limited value in AI/ML manuscripts that do not share data/code, because AI/ML methodologies and computational workflows are complex (require many different tools and make many assumptions)... thus making results difficult to reproduce and extend from the manuscript alone. If your data/code are proprietary, you can generate representative/tutorial examples that help readers understand your implementation and assumptions.

You are saving readers of your manuscript a lot of time by providing your data/code and this will make your work more impactful.
