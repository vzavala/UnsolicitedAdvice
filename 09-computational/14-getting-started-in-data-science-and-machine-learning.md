Was chatting with a student that is new to the area of data science and machine learning about how to get immersed in this ever-expanding  universe.

It is quite easy to get lost in this space, as there are many techniques and variants. My advice is to identify the techniques that best exemplify the fundamentals needed. You do not have to master them all at the beginning,  just need to understand what is around and what types of problems different techniques address.
