# Algorithms Don't Understand Physics

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists doing computational modeling.* Posted on LinkedIn on [May 15, 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7196324404492988416/).

Remember that numerical algorithms are mathematical tools that do not understand physics.

Newton’s method can converge to spurious (non-physical) solutions that satisfy your set of equations and neural networks and optimization algorithms can “bend” the laws of physics in order to fit your data or find an optimum. 

Do not trust solutions delivered by an algorithm blindly. A lot of effort has to be spent to validate that the solution makes physical and practical sense. In addition, it is important to understand/explain why the algorithm is selecting a particular solution. 

I also think that it is important to remember that certain aspects arising in real applications cannot be easily expressed mathematically (fairness, sustainability, consensus, happiness, risk, human behavior). As such, solutions delivered by an algorithm need to be scrutinized.
