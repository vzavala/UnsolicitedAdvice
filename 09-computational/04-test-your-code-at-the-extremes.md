# Test Your Code at the Extremes

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists doing computational research.* Posted on LinkedIn on [April 16, 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7185793573873676291/).

Always test your code/model under "extreme" scenarios. Ensuring that your implementation works under "average" scenarios is necessary, but not particularly informative. It is at the extremes where you will find interesting behavior that might help you identify weak points and that might help you refine your implementation to make it more robust, scalable, and efficient. 

Designing extreme scenarios for testing is not as easy as it sounds, you need to have solid working knowledge of your code/model to anticipate where its weak points might be. Don't be afraid to "break" your code/model; by breaking it, you will find ways to make it better.
