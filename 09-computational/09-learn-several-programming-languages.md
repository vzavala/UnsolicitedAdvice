# Learn Several Programming Languages

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists working on computational modeling.* Posted on LinkedIn on [May 29, 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7201603997005197314/).

There are many benefits of learning multiple programming languages. Just as with spoken languages, sometimes we get too comfortable with one language because this is what we are familiar with or because most people use it. 

However, you can learn a lot about your "core" language by comparing similarities/differences and advantages/disadvantages with other languages. I also think that knowing multiple languages helps develop problem-solving and abstraction skills, as it helps you identify common patterns and clever ways to combine them. You do not need to master every language to be able to do this. 

I've always been fascinated by spoken languages, trying to understand their benefits/drawbacks, where do they come from, how they have developed based on historical needs, and how they connect. There are a lot of similarities with programming languages.
