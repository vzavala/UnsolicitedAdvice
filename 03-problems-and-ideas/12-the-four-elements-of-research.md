# The Four Elements of Research

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [April 24, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7321310403261566980/).

I had a really nice conversation with grad students   about academia this week and one of them broke down beautifully the key elements of scientific research:

1) Select a problem to solve 
2) Gain the skills necessary to solve the problem
3) Get the results
4) Communicate the results 

We discussed how 1) is by far the most difficult aspect of research. This is a skill that can be developed by rigorously following the scientific method (e.g., What question am I answering? What is the hypothesis? What is the data/evidence?) and by listening to stakeholders that might be interested in your research (e.g., What does society or industry need?)
