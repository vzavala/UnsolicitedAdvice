# Find Inspiration in Simple Places

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [August 11, 2023](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7095587760631152640/).

Try to find inspiration for your research (no matter how complex it seems) in simple places (e.g., nature, home, sports, daily life, art). 

The governing principles of things (e.g., collective behavior, self-organization, energy minimization, stability) are often simple and are everywhere. It is important for you to develop the ability to spot these principles; this can help spark your creativity, think abstractly, deepen your understanding, and find creative new approaches to communicate your work.
