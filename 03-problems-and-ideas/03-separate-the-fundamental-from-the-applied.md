# Separate the Fundamental from the Applied

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [August 24, 2023](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7100270428811657216/).

When exploring a research topic, try to separate the “applied” aspects from the “fundamental” aspects.

The fundamental aspects/principles are things that you can likely transfer to other applications; as such, try to focus on mastering those. I think this is relevant because you will see that science experiences a lot of “swings”; for example, applications that were relevant 10 years ago, might not be that relevant anymore… but the fundamental principles are often quite stable and remain relevant.
