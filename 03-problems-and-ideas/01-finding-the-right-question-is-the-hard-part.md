# Finding the Right Question Is the Hard Part

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [February 21, 2023](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7033806014571311104/).

When I was in my PhD, I thought that my goal was (just) to solve problems. Later in my postdoc I recognized that solving problems is hard but finding the right problems to solve is even harder. 

Since then I have developed the habit of stop asking myself "What is the answer?" and instead asking myself "What is the question?" or "Is this question really worth answering?" Developing this way of thinking was really difficult but over time it has become more natural.
