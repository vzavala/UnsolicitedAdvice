# Make Time for Focused Thinking

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [December 26, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7410353066903830528/).

When I was in my PhD, a professor told me "You want to allocate time to think, just good old, focused thinking."

Over time I have learned to appreciate the relevance of this piece of advice. It is difficult to allocate significant chunks of time during our daily routines for focused thinking/ideation/reflection. We spend so much time executing thousands of tasks; this introduces a lot of noise into our brains. Our brains get pulled into so many directions that sometimes I feel I end up making semi-random decisions.

We think and generate ideas all the time, of course, but focused thinking is a different experience. Sitting down for 1 hr generating and evaluating ideas can do a lot for us; it helps us go deep and cut through the noise to identify meaningful directions. Grabbing a notepad and brainstorming ideas is healthy and helps us foster/reinforce our creative thinking skills. Generating ideas is a skill that you can develop and there is science behind this. It is also good to reflect about why we are doing what we are doing, as a way to evaluate what other paths we might be able to take and get out of rabbit holes.
