# Connect with Seemingly Unrelated Fields

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [September 28, 2023](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7113304717685911553/).

Challenge yourself to establish collaborations with (or connections to) fields that do not appear to be directly related to your field. Finding those non-obvious points of connection will help you gain appreciation for the applicability of your research/skills and will help you understand the unique perspectives that you bring to the table. Also, at a fundamental level, all areas of science are connected via the same governing principles (and it is good to develop the ability to identify such principles). 

One of the most exciting parts of being a scientist is finding opportunities for collaboration.
