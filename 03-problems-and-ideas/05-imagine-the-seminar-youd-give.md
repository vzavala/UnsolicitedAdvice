# Imagine the Seminar You'd Give

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [February 22, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7299087666808008706/).

Sometimes I like to plan/guide a research direction by trying to imagine myself giving a seminar on this topic. 

What would be the story that I tell people? Would the questions be intriguing? Would the findings/results be exciting? Would I feel proud of giving a talk on this subject?

Of course, one cannot anticipate the contents of a talk without having actual results, but this thought process is a "reality check" that helps me be honest with myself on whether the questions/topics that I am after (and potential results) would be interesting/exciting/useful to people. 

I think this type of reflection can also be useful for getting us out of "rabbit holes" (directions that might not be particularly useful).
