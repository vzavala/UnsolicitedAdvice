# Self-Check Lists

Come back to these every few months.

## For PhD students and postdocs

**Well-being**
- [ ] Did I rest enough this week, and did I stop when I hit my daily goal?
- [ ] Do I know the warning signs of burnout or depression, and who I'd talk to?
- [ ] Am I tracking my own progress instead of comparing myself with others?
- [ ] After a stressful stretch ends, do I expect a dip (the let-down effect) and plan some recovery?
- [ ] Do I have a way to clear my head, like running or a walk?

**Research**
- [ ] Have I spent time on focused thinking about *which* question to pursue?
- [ ] Have I set aside time to just play with ideas?
- [ ] Are my ideas written down and prioritized?
- [ ] Could I give an exciting seminar on the direction I'm taking?
- [ ] When a paper, proposal or experiment fails, do I learn from it and move on to the next point?
- [ ] When I use AI tools, do I understand and check how they reached the answer?

**Communication and writing**
- [ ] Could I explain my work without jargon, in a way people can relate to?
- [ ] For my next talk: did I sketch the story before making slides?
- [ ] Am I writing in small wins, and celebrating them?
- [ ] For one of my first talks: did I write out a script and practice it?
- [ ] When I visit a university, lab or company, did I do my homework on the people and the place first?

**Organization**
- [ ] Do I have clear milestones with real deadlines?
- [ ] Am I taking good notes in technical meetings?
- [ ] Am I saying no to some requests, so I can say yes to what matters?
- [ ] Am I making figures, code and slides I'll be able to reuse?

**People and career**
- [ ] Do I have several mentors besides my advisor?
- [ ] Am I building skills beyond research, like teaching, leadership and collaboration?
- [ ] Do I know what I want to be known for?
- [ ] Do my mentors and I trust each other to be open, reliable and responsive?
- [ ] When impostor feelings show up, do I treat them as normal and talk about them?
- [ ] Am I taking criticism as feedback on the work, not on me?

**Computational work**
- [ ] Did I work through a simple version by hand before computing?
- [ ] Did I build the model step by step, and test it at the extremes?
- [ ] Do the results make physical sense, and can I explain why the algorithm chose them?
- [ ] Is my model as complex as it needs to be, and no more, for the insight I want?
- [ ] Am I sharing my data and code with my papers?

## For faculty

- [ ] Is my research time blocked off and protected?
- [ ] Have I done some hands-on research, or read for pleasure, recently?
- [ ] Is the level of freedom I give each student right for their stage?
- [ ] Am I trusting my students and giving honest feedback with care?
- [ ] Are my projects good training for my students?
- [ ] Am I helping each mentee find their own strengths and build confidence?
- [ ] Does each student know how their work fits into the bigger picture?
- [ ] Am I teaching students to handle problems that aren't well defined?
- [ ] Is my research program built around a core of fundamental directions?
- [ ] Am I saying no often enough to protect my time and energy?
- [ ] Have I organized something recently that brings the community together?
- [ ] Have I looked back recently and celebrated what the group has achieved?
