# Preface

People often ask me why I write these posts and where the ideas come from. The honest answer is that I try to remember things I wish someone had told me when I was younger, particularly when I was struggling. Many of these things seem basic, and we take them for granted, but they can make a big difference when you're starting a career in science.

I never planned for this to become a series. It grew because people kept telling me it helped. At conferences, students, postdocs and professors stopped me to say they appreciated the "unsolicited advice." One message said every post had been "either a great wake-up call, a good nudge, or a much needed reminder." Messages like that are why I keep going, and why I decided to collect the posts in one place.

The advice comes from my own path: from Mexico to a PhD in the United States, a postdoc and research at a national laboratory, and a faculty career. None of it is a rule. Use what helps, ignore what doesn't, and find your own way. Above all, I hope this book reminds you that you're not alone and that doing science should be fun.

This book is dedicated to all my mentees and mentors, who have made me a better person and scientist. 

*Victor M. Zavala*

*Madison, Wisconsin*
