# Unsolicited Advice for Young Scientists

**A handbook for PhD students, postdocs and young faculty**

*Victor M. Zavala*

---

Between 2022 and 2026, I wrote a series of short LinkedIn posts that all began the same way: *"Unsolicited advice for young scientists…"* They're the things I wish someone had told me when I was starting out, especially when I was struggling. Many seem basic, but they can make a big difference early in a career.

This book collects all 82 posts and groups them by theme instead of by date. Each part opens with its key takeaways, followed by the original posts.

## The big ideas

1. **Your brain is your main tool, so protect it.** Rest makes you more productive, and mental health deserves attention.
2. **Asking the right question matters more than answering one.** Make time to think, and aim for work that opens new problems.
3. **Communication is part of the science.** Ideas only have impact if they're shared, and shared well.
4. **The process matters more than the outcome.** Failures, rejections and pushback are where learning happens.
5. **People are what make you effective.** Mentors, networks, self-awareness and humility matter.
6. **Structure supports creativity.** Plan, write things down, and treat what you produce as an investment.
7. **Reflect on purpose and progress.** Know what you want to be known for, and celebrate what you've done.
