# Be an Open and Confident Communicator

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [May 15, 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7461082744924770305/).

Learn to be an open and confident communicator. 

If you disagree with something, express it. If you agree with something, express it. If you like a seminar, tell the speaker. If you do not like a seminar, provide constructive criticism. If you are struggling with a problem, express it. If you failed, express it. If you were successful, express it. 

Science is all about exchange of ideas and open dialogue. We become better scientists when we communicate openly. We are all wired in one giant neural network, but the neurons cannot connect unless we speak openly and express our ideas, failures, and successes.
