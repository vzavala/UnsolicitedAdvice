# Science Is About Selling Ideas

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [December 12, 2023](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7140169353571766275/).

Don't forget that science is about generating and *selling* ideas; a great idea needs to be socialized/communicated effectively for it to propagate and make tangible impact. You need to sell your ideas to the scientific community, to funding agencies, or to your boss inside a company (e.g., so that you can justify why your idea is worth pursuing or investing on). 

Teaching/outreach is also about selling ideas; you need to convince your audience (students or the public) that the concepts presented are interesting/useful or they will not remain engaged and learn. 

How do you sell ideas effectively? There is no easy answer... communication is an art and a science. Take courses on (or read about) technical communication, visualization, cognitive psychology, storytelling, marketing, ..., even comedy (Why not?). Pay attention to the response of your audience, get feedback, and think about creative/alternative ways of communicating your content. Experimenting with communication strategies can be fun.
