# Include a Personal Intro Slide

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [November 23, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7398459084255064065/).

It is useful to include a personal intro slide in your presentations. 

- It makes you sound more personable/approachable/human; as a result, the audience will be more open to interact with you and to ask questions.

- It helps you connect with the audience at a deeper level. People will connect with you based on facts you share with them; e.g., people often stop by after my talks to share that they also love Tool :)

- It helps the audience understand your background; this can help them understand where your perceptions might be coming from. 

- It helps students visualize your career path; this can help demystify the process of becoming a scientist (or a professional). 

The first time I did this (many years ago), I was afraid that this was going to come across as "unprofessional." But I decided to try and I have been pleasantly surprised by how receptive people (particularly students) are of this practice.  

You can always share as much detail as you feel comfortable or as you feel is appropriate based on context. Remember that there is such a thing as TMI :)
