# A Poster Without the Poster Tube

> *More advice (not part of the series).* Posted on LinkedIn on [June 30, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7345252587606069250/).

If you do not want to carry a poster tube across the Atlantic ocean (and have airport security giving you weird looks), you can simply print slides and paste them on the board as a collage. 

The printed slides are much easier to carry (in a folder), you can print them in nice glossy paper, and you basically get a similar effect as a  standard poster (as below). In addition, you can just reuse slides that that you have from previous presentations.

I tried this approach the first time because I forgot my poster tube in an airplane on my way to a conference. I did not have time to find a poster printer so I just printed some slides at the hotel and was ready to go. 

I do think that posters that are tailored-made are super nice and look more professional, but they are difficult to carry around and I often do not have much time to design them.
