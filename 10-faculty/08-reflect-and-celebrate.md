# Reflect and Celebrate

> *Unsolicited advice for young Faculty.* Posted on LinkedIn on [August 17, 2023](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7097915431348289537/).

Take some time every week or month to reflect on all the things that you are accomplishing.

It is really easy in this job to get ultra busy with all the teaching, research, fundraising, service, outreach, administration,... that we have to do and we can lose track of all the great things that we are doing for our students and our communities. As you push forward, don't forget to look back from time to time and celebrate your successes (even if "minor").
