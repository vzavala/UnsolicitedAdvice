# Teach Students to Handle Ill-Defined Problems

> *More advice (not part of the series).* Posted on LinkedIn on [February 20, 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7430427919543468032/).

In the era of AI, we should be teaching students to deal with problems that are not well defined. If the problem is fully defined, then the solution is almost mechanical and AI will solve it in no time. 

AI still struggles to deal with problems that are ambiguous; it is here where expert judgement is needed to make suitable assumptions. Such problems do not have unique solutions, have missing data, and involve complex trade-offs… those are the types of problems that you encounter in real life.
