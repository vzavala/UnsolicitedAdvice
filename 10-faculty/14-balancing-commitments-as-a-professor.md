# Balancing Commitments as a Professor

> Posted on LinkedIn on [December 10, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7404530198710530048/).

One of the most challenging aspects of being a Professor is balancing lots of different tasks/commitments. 

It is inevitable that you will have to say NO a lot and it is often difficult to say NO to some requests (due to a variety of factors). 

At least for me personally, this continuous balancing act makes feel that I am always disappointing someone and it stresses me out. Over time I have gotten better at dealing with this feeling; I try to remind myself that I am trying my best to be fair, that people are actually quite forgiving and are in the same boat as me, and that not everything is urgent/critical.
