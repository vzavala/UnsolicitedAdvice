# Designing Problems for Your Courses

> Posted on LinkedIn on [February 19, 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7165149441627242496/).

Designing problems for your courses can be super challenging but also super rewarding. 

You need to carefully balance difficulty (you want the problem challenging but not impossible) and relevance (you want it realistic but not too real), you want it puzzling but not confusing, you want it open-ended but not ambiguous, you need to hit learning outcomes, and you need to think about the background of the students. This is part science and part art :) 

At times you identify interesting research ideas when designing some of these problems because, implicitly, you are framing questions. Just Today I designed a problem by framing a question for which I have no answer. Designing problems also helps you communicate your research, as you need to distill complex ideas down to key concepts.
