# Why Research Mentoring Matters

> Posted on LinkedIn on [May 6, 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7193234773555752960/).

Why is effective research mentoring essential? 

- If you want to solve difficult scientific problems, you want to surround yourself with talented and motivated people.
- If you want to solve difficult scientific problems, you want to identify and develop the unique talents of your people. 
- If you want to solve difficult scientific problems, you want to sustain the motivation of your people.
