# Keep Doing Actual Research

> *Unsolicited advice for young faculty.* Posted on LinkedIn on [December 9, 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7271905889060577280/).

As you know, faculty life can be super demanding with teaching, service, mentoring, committees, events, travel, reporting, proposal-writing, ....

With all these activities it is easy to forget to dedicate time to do one of the things that we love and enjoy the most... doing actual scientific research (e.g., read papers/books, code, do calculations/math, get in the lab). 

I try to allocate some fraction of my time every week/month for this; yes, it is often challenging, but I always find it reenergizing when I use some solid block of time to do actual research.
