# Organize Events

> *Unsolicited advice for young professors.* Posted on LinkedIn on [August 27, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7366455028196233216/).

Organizing events (e.g., workshops, short courses, outreach, conferences) is a scalable and effective approach to connect you to the scientific community and has many important benefits such as: gaining ideas/perspectives/feedback to help you and attendees frame their future research, establish partnerships and collaborations, raise the visibility of your students/trainees, and disseminate knowledge.

Organizing events requires a lot of time and careful planning, but this is an investment that pays off in the long run. Every event that I have organized has been amazingly rewarding; it is nice to see an event unfolding in real time, generating ideas and excitement, and impacting people.
