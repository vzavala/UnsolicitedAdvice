# Professors Need Some Psychology

> *Unsolicited advice for young faculty.* Posted on LinkedIn on [March 31, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7312505458143940608/).

I went to school to study chemical engineering but I always knew that I could have also studied psychology. 

As a professor, it is remarkable how much psychology I actually use in teaching, mentoring, research, and management. 

It is important to be aware of the emotional status of students and colleagues, understand how people cope with trauma/stress,  understand how to motivate people (e.g., based on their personality types), and understand the different thought processes that people go through based on their age, gender, or  backgrounds (e.g., a 50 yr old professor and an 18 yr old student have very different priorities and perceptions of life).

I do think that it is extremely helpful to have some basic notions of psychology in our professional lives. This becomes clear as your management roles become more prominent.
