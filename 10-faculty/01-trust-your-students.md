# Trust Your Students

> *Unsolicited advice for young faculty.* Posted on LinkedIn on [July 6, 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7479924879387992064/).

Trust your students/mentees; allow them to be themselves and to make mistakes. Try to guide them, but try not force your worldview or your ways to do things on them (do not control them). Trusting their abilities/judgement and giving them flexibility is what helps them build confidence. 

Also, treat your students/mentees as professionals, because that is what they are. When people are treated as professionals, they will behave as professionals. When people are treated as children, they will behave as children.
