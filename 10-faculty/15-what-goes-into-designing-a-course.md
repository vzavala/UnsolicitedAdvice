# What Goes into Designing a Course

> *More advice (not part of the series).* Posted on LinkedIn on [October 26, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7388310211377315840/).

Amazing how much time goes into designing a course and all the factors that one needs to consider when doing this. This becomes easier with experience but still requires significant effort. 

Some of the factors that I normally think about are:

- What learning outcomes do we need to hit?
- What are the topics that I need to cover and how  are they interrelated (to ensure logical flow)?
- How much lecture time do I have? How to assign topics to lectures?
- How to evaluate (e.g., HWs, exams, projects, quizzes)?
- How to spread the workload so that students do not get saturated?
- What examples and strategies can I use to keep students engaged?
- How to manage teams?

And that is just the design, the execution is substantially more difficult :)
