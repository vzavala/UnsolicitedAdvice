# Write Proposals for the Ideas, Not the Money

> *Unsolicited advice for young Faculty.* Posted on LinkedIn on [December 6, 2022](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7005893497190830080/).

Writing proposals can be difficult and stressful. To get yourself motivated, try to make it less about the money that you expect to obtain and more about the process of generating/refining ideas and establishing collaborations. I know, it is difficult when you need the money, but try. 

I have been writing proposals for 10+ years now; I can tell you that I remember each proposal that I have written fondly (the funded and the unfunded ones). 

When I look back at the unfunded ones, I am not reminded of the failure, but of the key learnings and byproducts that came out of them. 

Some of those unfunded proposals sparked collaborations that I still have, some of them sparked ideas that became papers, some of them forced me to think in a different way. After you accumulate enough failures, you start seeing the benefits of this process in helping you develop your scientific career.
