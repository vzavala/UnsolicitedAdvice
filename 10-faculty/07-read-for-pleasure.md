# Read for Pleasure

> *Unsolicited advice for young professors.* Posted on LinkedIn on [July 11, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7349508927132233728/).

Take some time off to enjoy reading a paper/book or to just enjoy the beauty of doing research and of generating ideas.

With the 1,000,000 things that we need to think/worry about (particularly in these pressing times), it is easy to forget what this job is about. 

I don't think that I have sit down and read a paper/book for pleasure for months... it feels so nice to be able to do this, it reminds me of being a PhD student.
