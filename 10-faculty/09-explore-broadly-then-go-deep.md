# Explore Broadly, Then Go Deep

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [March 10, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7304850400275243008/).

A common challenge in establishing a research program is to figure out how wide/deep you want to go in your research directions. You want to have a suitable trade-off of breadth and depth. 

When I was starting my research career, a senior colleague recommended that I should go out, explore, and be open minded about what research directions to pursue (even if these were not in my original plan). He also told me that, once I became more established, I could be more selective and pick the best ones to pursue deeper. This contradicted what I originally had in mind (focus and go deep on a few directions).

I think that was the best advice that anyone gave me early on. Being open minded helped me work on things that I did not expect to be so productive and interesting. This also helped me gain knowledge and skills that I did not expect, and helped me establish collaborations that continue to be productive. Moreover, this helped me appreciate how my skills could be used in a variety if fields.
