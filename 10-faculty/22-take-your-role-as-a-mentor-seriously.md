# Take Your Role as a Mentor Seriously

> *More advice (not part of the series).* Posted on LinkedIn on [March 21, 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7176563527942303745/).

As mentors/educators, we have the ability to influence the course of the professional and personal lives of our mentees/students. As such, we should take our role seriously.
