# Mentoring Is an Emotional Investment

> *More advice (not part of the series).* Posted on LinkedIn on [June 1, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7335050741960626176/).

Mentorship is not only an investment of time, it is also an investment of emotional energy.

One worries quite a bit when our mentees are struggling, and we need to think carefully about how to best help them.

At the same time, one feels extremely satisfied/happy when our mentees are doing well. 

There is a strong emotional bond between a mentor and a mentee, and this lasts forever. This is one of the most rewarding aspects of mentorship.
