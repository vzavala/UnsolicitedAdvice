# The Best Mentors Genuinely Care

> *Unsolicited advice for young faculty.* Posted on LinkedIn on [March 26, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7310483254921347072/).

When I look back at my professional career, it is clear that the best mentors that I have had are people that *genuinely* cared about my well-being (professional and personal). 

So much about mentorship is sincerely caring about others (and to show it explicitly).
