# Help Mentees Discover Their Strengths

> *More advice (not part of the series).* Posted on LinkedIn on [June 21, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7342303069273001985/).

One of the main goals of PhD mentorship is to help mentees discover what their skills, passions, and career goals are.

This is not trivial and requires continuous dialogue, evaluation, and feedback. 

Specifically, mentees change their perceptions/priorities as they progress in their studies, as they mature both personally and professionally, as they become aware of the different ways of conducting research that exist, and as they become aware of the diverse career pathways that exist. 

Also, sometimes our skills are not obvious to us and we might require external validation/perspective from an expert to become aware of them. I still remember one of my mentors once telling me that I was a good “mathematical modeler”; this is something that had never really crossed my mind up to that point.
