# Great Ideas Often Meet Resistance

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [August 22, 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7232189382114304000/).

I was talking Today to one of my trainees about how new ideas and ways of thinking can be received with a lot of resistance.

We have the perception that great ideas will be received with immediate praise and positivity. However, great ideas are also often received with resistance and skepticism. This can be very disheartening or embarassing, as you might feel that you are doing something wrong/dumb.

However, resistance can also be a manifestation that you touched a “nerve” and challenged people to think in a different way (which can make people uncomfortable). Be patient, it takes time to convince people that your ideas and ways of thinking are worth pursuing.

In fact, I would argue that ideas generate the most impact when one consistently works on refining them and on educating others on how to use them.
