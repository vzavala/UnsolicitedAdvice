# You Try, You Fail, You Try Again

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [December 30, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7411772897205186560/).

This is the list of (some) of my research proposals submitted in 2025. 

You try your best, you fail often, you (might) win some, you learn/refine, and you try again. 

Selling and promoting your ideas is never easy (in academia, in business, or in life in general). Sometimes factors need to align and sometimes you just get lucky.  

No doubt that we are living in difficult times; it is easy to get discouraged by our environment, but we have limited control over that. What is in our control is our ability to continue to push forward and to try our best.
