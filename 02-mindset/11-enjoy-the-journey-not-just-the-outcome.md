# Enjoy the Journey, Not Just the Outcome

> Posted on LinkedIn on [February 21, 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7431001512870330368/).

The U.S. figure skating team has given us some important lessons about the importance of focusing on mental health and well-being. 

These athletes face so much scrutiny and pressure from media/society/family/sponsors. The amount of mental strength they need is outstanding; some of them, understandably, could not handle it. 

The most remarkable case for me is that of Alysa Liu; a young athlete that at some point quit her passion because of burn out and crushing pressure. She decided to come back a couple of years later and took a different mindset that focuses less on the outcomes and more on enjoying the journey. This shows a remarkable amount of emotional intelligence and maturity.

I feel so many of these lessons are applicable to scientists. With all the pressures we face, we tend to focus too much on outcomes and we often forget about enjoying the journey.
