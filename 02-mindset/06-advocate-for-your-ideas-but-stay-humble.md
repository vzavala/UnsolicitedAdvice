# Advocate for Your Ideas, but Stay Humble

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [December 30, 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7279494643593949184/).

At the core of being a scientist is to develop new approaches to tackle problems and to *advocate/justify/sell* why our approaches are better than the alternatives. 

Advocacy sometimes can lead us to take rather extreme/unhealthy competitive views on the subject; for example, we can tend to promote our approach too hard and disregard the alternatives (it is my way or the highway). I have seen this happening over and over again in science and this is something that I am not particularly fond of. 

Having diverse views on problems is what makes science advance; no silver bullet exists to tackle problems. You might disagree with a specific approach/view on a subject, but we should stay humble and always acknowledge the views and contributions of everyone... e.g., with time we might discover that our approaches/views turn out to be limited or wrong :)
