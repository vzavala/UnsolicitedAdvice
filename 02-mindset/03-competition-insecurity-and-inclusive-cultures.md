# Competition, Insecurity and Inclusive Cultures

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [August 19, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7363539502956498945/).

Highly competitive environments tend to trigger insecurities and comparisons: "This person is smarter/better than me", "I am never going to be as good as them", ... 

I have personally experienced these types of thoughts, particularly when I was a student, because I used to unfairly compare myself with senior students that had a lot more experience and to students in other research areas that required drastically different skillsets. 

One way to control these feelings is to create inclusive environments that acknowledge & celebrate the successes/contributions of *all* our colleagues. When we acknowledge one another, we let go of our egos and we face our fears/insecurities head on. 

Also, as people often say, there is always going to be someone better than you. I think the important thing is to look internally (as opposed to externally) into what we contribute, what excites us, and what makes us happy.
