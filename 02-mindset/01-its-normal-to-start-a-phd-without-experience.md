# It's Normal to Start a PhD Without Experience

> *Unsolicited advice for young scientists.* Posted on LinkedIn on [March 15, 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7306686429638574080/).

It is normal to start your PhD with limited (or no) prior experience on the subject. 

I have seen this over and over again; some of my best PhD students (focus on computation) conducted experimental research in their undergrad and had limited computational experience. 

During your PhD, you will focus on a specific set of topics and, as a result, you will learn and acquire the skills necessary very quickly. 

In a PhD, curiosity and desire to learn are more important than prior experience.
